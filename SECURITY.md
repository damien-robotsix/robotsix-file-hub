# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it by emailing
[admin@robotsix.net](mailto:admin@robotsix.net). Please do not disclose
the issue publicly until it has been addressed.

We are committed to coordinated disclosure: we will work with you to
understand and resolve the issue before any public announcement.

## Response Time

We aim to acknowledge receipt of your report within a reasonable
response time and will keep you informed of our progress toward a fix.
